# DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

## Colors
- **Background**: #000000 (pure black)
- **Primary**: #10B981 (emerald green)
- **Secondary**: #06B6D4 (cyan)
- **Accent**: #A855F7 (purple for Range)
- **Text**: #FFFFFF, #A1A1AA (zinc for secondary)

## Fonts
- **Headlines**: Space Grotesk or JetBrains Mono (bold)
- **Body**: Inter or DM Sans
- **Code/Terminal**: Fira Code or JetBrains Mono

## Effects
- Subtle glow on key elements
- Terminal-style boxes with ASCII borders
- "Redacted" effect: black bars over hidden data
- Scanline overlay (subtle, 5% opacity)
